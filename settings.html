Role Permissions

Admin

  • Full access to all pages
  • Manage staff accounts
  • Manage site settings
  • View revenue & earnings
  • Edit any booking (any status)
  • Revert completed/canceled

Supervisor

  • Edit any booking (any status)
  • Revert completed/canceled
  • Cancel bookings
  • Mark bookings complete
  • Cannot view revenue/earnings
  • Cannot manage users
  • Cannot access site settings

Staff

  • Create bookings
  • Edit pending/upcoming/ongoing
  • Cancel & mark complete
  • Cannot edit complete/canceled
  • Cannot view revenue/earnings
  • Cannot manage users
  • Cannot access site settings
